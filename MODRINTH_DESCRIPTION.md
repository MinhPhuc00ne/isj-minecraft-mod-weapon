> **Weapons Mod** is an ultimate mythical armament & anime-inspired power fantasy mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric)**. Wield the legendary **Infinity Gauntlet** to manipulate space-time and obliterate foes with a snap, awaken as a **True Demon Lord** through the Harvest Festival, and master supreme holy skills with the **Moonlight Ritual Sword**!

---

## 📖 In-Game Guide: Celestial Tome (Thánh Thư Thần Khí)

Never get lost! The mod includes a built-in interactive visual guide book — **Celestial Tome**:
- **Right-Click** the book to open the illustrated manual.
- Browse all crafting recipes, evolution requirements, active skills, and controls with clickable tabs and intuitive pagination.

---

## 🌟 Core Features & Modules

### 🥊 1. The Infinity Gauntlet & 6 Cosmic Stones
*Craft the golden gauntlet, embed the cosmic stones, and command omnipotence:*
- **Empty Gauntlet**: Forged with Netherite and Gold ingots.
- **6 Infinity Stones**: Collect **Power, Space, Reality, Soul, Time, and Mind** stones.
- **Dynamic Mode Switch (`Page Up`)**: Open an interactive GUI to socket gems or select active stone powers.
- **Immunity & God Mode**: Holding the loaded gauntlet grants complete damage immunity, lava/drowning immunity, and rapid health regeneration.
- **The Decimation Snap**: Shift + Right-Click to execute the legendary Snap — eradicating hostile creatures and entities within a 100-block radius with async multithreading performance!

---

### ⚔️ 2. Moonlight Ritual Sword (Thanh Kiếm Nguyệt Quang)
*An ancient celestial blade boasting infinite durability and 5 god-tier active arts:*
- **Key [Z]**: Cycle between active mythical skills.
- **Right-Click**: Unleash the currently selected supreme art:
  1. ⚡ **Black Flash Conqueror Slash (Hắc Thiểm Bá Vương Trảm)**: Spatial-distorting crimson flash shockwave slicing through all enemies.
  2. 🌟 **Trinity Sanctuary (Disintegration)**: Summons a massive holy magic circle disintegrating targets at the molecular level.
  3. ☀️ **Jacob's Ladder**: Descends a column of celestial holy light purging unholy entities.
  4. 🌌 **Void King Beelzebuth**: Unleashes the dragon void maw consuming everything in its path.
  5. 🐉 **Drago-Nova (Long Tinh Phách Tinh Bạo)**: The apex cosmic explosion skill (*Unlocked when wearing the Full Divine Armament + True Demon Lord status*).

---

### 👑 3. True Demon Lord Awakening (Tensura Evolution)
*Ascend beyond humanity and undergo the Harvest Festival:*
- **Demon Lord Seed**: Infuse dark demonic energy once you reach Level 10+ EXP.
- **Soul Harvest**: Slain 10,000 souls from hostile creatures to incubate the seed.
- **Awakening**: Right-click the ripened seed to trigger the **Harvest Festival Evolution**:
  - Max Health boosted to **40 Hearts (80 HP)**.
  - Permanent **Speed** and **Resistance** blessings.
  - Unarmed combat arts: Cast predatory skills even without holding weapons.

---

### 🛡️ 4. Divine Armament Armor (Bộ Giáp Thần Thoại)
*Forged from transcendent starlight materials:*
- Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, and Boots providing unmatched armor toughness, knockback resistance, and damage reduction.
- Full Set Bonus: Unlocks the supreme cosmic technique **Drago-Nova** when wielding the Moonlight Sword as a True Demon Lord.

## 🎮 Keybindings & Controls

| Keybind | Action | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`Z`** | **Cycle Active Skill** | Switches between Moonlight Sword arts and Demon Lord skills |
| **`Right-Click`** | **Cast Skill / Read Book** | Casts the active skill or opens the Celestial Tome |
| **`Page Up`** | **Infinity Gauntlet Menu** | Opens GUI to socket gems or switch Infinity Stone modes |
| **`Shift + Right-Click`** | **Cosmic Snap** | Triggers the 100-block radius Snap with the full Gauntlet |

## 📦 Requirements & Dependencies

To play **Weapons Mod**, ensure you have installed:
- **Minecraft**: `1.21.1`
- **Mod Loader**: [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/) (`>= 0.16.0`)
- **[Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)** (`>= 0.116.17+1.21.1`) *(Required)*
- **[Architectury API (Fabric)](https://modrinth.com/mod/architectury-api)** (`>= 13.0.8`) *(Required)*

---

## 📥 Installation

1. Download and install **Fabric Loader 1.21.1**.
2. Download the required dependencies: **Fabric API** and **Architectury API**.
3. Download **Weapons Mod** (`weapons-1.21.1-fabric-1.0.0.jar`).
4. Place all downloaded `.jar` files into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
5. Launch the game and enjoy!
